# Painted banner and rugged paper

The owner requested a hand-painted lotería-style logo over an arched, wavy ribbon and a rugged paper page background on 2026-10-27.

- Banner: `public/brand/loteria-banner.webp`, transparent, 1200 × 403, original generated artwork.
- Paper: `public/textures/rugged-paper.webp`, 640 × 640, tiled at 480 CSS pixels and multiplied with the existing theme background.
- Production used the built-in imagegen tool, then cwebp for delivery resizing/compression. No reference-deck artwork or trademarks are included.
- Home uses the large banner with an accessible name. Interior pages use a compact banner linking home. High-contrast mode omits texture.

Elegant joyful legible silhouette; painterly, not glossy, not modern geometric branding. No card illustrations, no rooster, no trademark symbols, no other brand names, no additional words. Keep empty surrounding area truly transparent, no shadow rectangle. All lettering readable at300px width.
